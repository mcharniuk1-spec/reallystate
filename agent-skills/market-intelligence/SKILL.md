# Market Intelligence

## Purpose

Use this skill for weekly market/rival analysis and strategic source/product recommendations.

## Required Inputs

- `deep-research-report.md`
- `data/source_registry.json`
- latest scraper/data analyst reports
- product UX docs
- cited public/official market evidence when external research is needed

## Workflow

1. Separate market facts from interpretation.
2. Track rival/source changes:
   - major portals
   - agencies
   - STR vendors
   - official/register channels
   - social/messenger lead surfaces
3. Summarize supply and price signals where reproducible data exists.
4. Recommend source priority changes only when evidence is strong.
5. Convert recommendations into planner-ready task proposals.

## Output

- Market/rival report.
- Source priority recommendations.
- Product/UX implications.
- Gaps and evidence limits.

## Safety

Do not recommend unauthorized scraping or private-channel automation.
